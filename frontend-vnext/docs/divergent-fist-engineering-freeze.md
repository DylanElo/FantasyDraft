# Divergent Fist engineering freeze

The isolated `/prototype/divergent-fist` combat-art integration scaffold is
engineering-frozen pending production art.

- Choreography is approved.
- Fighter formation and ground anchors are approved.
- Timing direction, including the physical/delayed two-hit rhythm, is approved.
- Laboratory architecture and deterministic camera contract are approved.
- The 47-slot asset contract is approved.
- No visual redesign should occur during the art drop.
- Production art may be integrated through the documented paths and production
  ID list without changing combat logic.
- Further engineering requires a concrete asset-driven need found during import,
  audit, or exact-viewport QA.

Freeze baseline commit: `TO_BE_RECORDED_AFTER_COMMIT`

This freeze does not claim the presentation is visually finished. Every
current runtime asset remains a placeholder until a validated, approved, and
rights-cleared production drop replaces it.
