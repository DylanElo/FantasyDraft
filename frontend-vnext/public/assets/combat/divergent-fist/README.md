# Divergent Fist placeholder assets

These files are technical blocking assets for `/prototype/divergent-fist`.
They are deliberately geometric and labeled. Fighter poses and effects use
transparent canvases; the sky is the only opaque plate. They are not final art,
are not approved for production, and must not be promoted as visual completion.

Replacement filenames, canvas sizes, anchors, facing rules, safe areas, and
layer requirements are defined in
`frontend-vnext/docs/divergent-fist-asset-manifest.md`.
